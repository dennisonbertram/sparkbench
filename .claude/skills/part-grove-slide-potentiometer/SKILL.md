# Grove - Slide Potentiometer (10 kΩ linear, 30 mm)

## Overview
The **Seeed Grove - Slide Potentiometer (SKU 101020036)** is a 30 mm-travel linear slide potentiometer (an S3033N-type 10 kΩ B-taper fader with a centre click) on a Grove board. It is a plain voltage divider:
- R1's track runs from GND to VCC and the wiper goes straight to **OUT** (Grove pin 1): V(OUT) = position × VCC, 0 V at the GND end of travel and VCC at the VCC end (both ends are marked on the board).
- Grove pin 2 (**LED**) drives an on-board green LED through a series resistor to GND. Drive it high from a GPIO to light it. It is an input to the module and is optional.
- Total resistance 10 kΩ ±20 %; residual resistance at the ends ≤ 20 Ω, so the ends read essentially 0 % and 100 %.

## Pinout (Grove connector, pin 1 to 4)
| Pin | Name | Wire colour | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OUT | yellow | Wiper output, 0 V to VCC (analog) |
| 2 | LED | white | Green indicator LED input, high = on (optional) |
| 3 | VCC | red | Top of the track, 3.3–5 V |
| 4 | GND | black | Bottom of the track, LED cathode |

Source: Seeed schematic (J1: 1 OUT, 2 LED, 3 VCC, 4 GND) and the Seeed wiki ("OUT (Pin 1), Vcc (Pin 3) and GND (Pin 4) ... Pin 2 is connected to an on-board green indicator LED").

## Electrical limits
- Supply 3.3–5 V (Seeed). OUT reaches VCC at full travel, so on an ESP32 or Pico power it from **3.3 V**.
- Track current VCC / 10 kΩ: 0.33 mA at 3.3 V. The LED draws a few mA from the LED pin when it is high.
- Pot rating (S3033N, B curve): 0.2 W single gang, 200 V AC max; far beyond logic supplies.
- Output source resistance up to 2.5 kΩ (slider in the middle); fine for MCU ADCs.

## Interface details
```
V(OUT)      = position × VCC
position(%) = 100 × V(OUT) / VCC
```
On 3.3 V: 0.33 V = 10 %, 1.155 V = 35 %, 1.65 V = 50 %, 3.3 V = 100 %.

## Wiring
Use a Grove-to-female-jumper cable, or a Grove shield analog port.

| Module | ESP32 | Raspberry Pi Pico | Raspberry Pi 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| OUT (yellow) | GPIO34 (ADC1) | GP26 / ADC0 | External ADC (Grove Base Hat A0, ADS1115) |
| LED (white) | any GPIO, optional | any GPIO, optional | any GPIO, optional |
| VCC (red) | 3V3 | 3V3(OUT) | 3.3 V (pin 1) |
| GND (black) | GND | GND | GND |

## Using the driver
```python
from grove_slide_pot import GroveSlidePot
import time
pot = GroveSlidePot(34, led=25)   # ADC GPIO for OUT, optional GPIO for the LED
while True:
    p = pot.percent()
    pot.led(p >= 50)
    print('P=%d%%' % round(p))
    time.sleep(0.5)
```
`volts()`, `fraction()` and `scaled(lo, hi)` are also available. Pass `vcc=` if the module is powered from something other than 3.3 V (and only if your ADC can take it).

## Gotchas
- **5 V on a 3.3 V board:** the wiper reaches VCC at the end of travel. Power it from 3V3.
- **LED pin is an input:** it lights the LED, it does not report anything. Leave it unconnected if you don't need it.
- **ESP32:** use ADC1 pins (GPIO32–39); ADC2 does not work while Wi-Fi is on. The ESP32 ADC is non-linear near 0 V and 3.3 V, so the ends may read a few % off.
- **Grove socket:** the board has a 2 mm Grove socket, not breadboard pins: use a Grove-to-jumper cable.
