# Slide potentiometer module (10 kΩ, B103)

## Overview
A **60 mm-travel linear slide potentiometer** (fader) on a small red PCB marked **"Slide Pot"**, sold as the "10K slide potentiometer module" for Arduino/ESP32 (e.g. ShillehTek "10K Slide Potentiometer Module"). The fader is dual-gang: two independent 10 kΩ linear (B103) carbon tracks moved by one lever. Each gang has its own 3-pin header: **OTA, VCC, GND** (channel A) and **OTB, VCC, GND** (channel B). There are no other components: it is a plain voltage divider.
- Output: V(OTA) = position × VCC (linear taper), 0 V at one end of travel, VCC at the other.
- Resistance tolerance ±20 %; residual (end) resistance up to 500 Ω or 1 %, so the ends may not read exactly 0 / 100 %.

Sparkbench models channel A's 3-pin header. Channel B's header (OTB, VCC, GND) is electrically identical and optional.

## Pinout (channel A header, in order)
| Pin | Name | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | OTA | Wiper output, 0 V to VCC (analog) |
| 2 | VCC | Top of the track, 3.3 V or 5 V |
| 3 | GND | Bottom of the track |

Source: board silkscreen (header groups "OTA VCC GND" and "OTB VCC GND") and the ShillehTek product manual.

## Electrical limits
- Supply: any voltage your ADC accepts, 3.3 V or 5 V. Use 3.3 V on an ESP32 or Pico: the wiper reaches VCC at full travel.
- Current: VCC / 10 kΩ, 0.33 mA at 3.3 V (0.5 mA at 5 V) per gang.
- Power rating (60 mm dual gang, linear): 0.125 W per element; maximum 200 V DC (Bourns PTA series). Far beyond any logic supply.
- Operating temperature −10 to +50 °C (PTA series).

## Interface details
```
V(OTA)      = position × VCC
position(%) = 100 × V(OTA) / VCC
```
On a 3.3 V supply: 0.33 V = 10 %, 1.65 V = 50 %, 2.31 V = 70 %, 3.3 V = 100 %.

## Wiring
| Module | ESP32 | Raspberry Pi Pico | Raspberry Pi 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| OTA | GPIO34 (ADC1) | GP26 / ADC0 | ADS1115 A0 (the Pi has no ADC) |
| VCC | 3V3 | 3V3(OUT) | 3.3 V (pin 1) |
| GND | GND | GND | GND |

Keep all three wires on the same labelled group (OTA with the VCC/GND next to it).

## Using the driver
```python
from slidepot import SlidePot
import time
pot = SlidePot(34)            # GPIO number of the ADC pin OTA is wired to
while True:
    print('P=%d%%' % round(pot.percent()))
    time.sleep(0.5)
```
`volts()`, `fraction()` and `scaled(lo, hi)` are also available. Pass `vcc=` if the module is powered from something other than 3.3 V (and only if your ADC can take it).

## Gotchas
- **5 V on a 3.3 V board:** the wiper reaches 5 V at the end of travel and can damage the ADC pin. Power it from 3V3.
- **ESP32:** use ADC1 pins (GPIO32–39); ADC2 does not work while Wi-Fi is on. The ESP32 ADC is non-linear near 0 V and 3.3 V, so the ends read a few % off.
- **Mixed groups:** taking VCC/GND from one header and the wiper from the other gives a reading from the other gang. It still works only because both gangs are powered.
- **Jitter:** carbon tracks are noisy (sliding noise up to 100 mV); the driver averages 16 samples.
