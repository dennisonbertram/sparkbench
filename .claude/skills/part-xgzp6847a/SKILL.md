# XGZP6847A gauge pressure sensor (CFSensor, analog)

## Overview
The **XGZP6847A** (CFSensor) is a silicon piezoresistive pressure sensor with a calibration ASIC on a small board, sold as a 6-pin **DIP6 "pressure sensor module"** with one 3 mm hose barb on top. It measures **gauge pressure** (relative to the air around it) and gives a calibrated, temperature-compensated, **ratiometric analog voltage**. The pressure range is part of the model number; this part is modelled as the common **XGZP6847A040KPG**:
- Range: **0 to 40 kPa** gauge (0 to 5.8 psi, about 0 to 4 m of water)
- Output: **0.5 V at 0 kPa to 4.5 V at 40 kPa** with a 5 V supply (100 mV per kPa)
- Transfer function: **Vout = VDD × (0.1 + 0.8 × P / 40 kPa)**
- Accuracy ±1.0 % span (≥ 20 kPa ranges), compensated 0 to 60 °C
- Supply: **5 V**, 3 mA max

Other ranges use the same pins and formula with a different full scale: 005KPG = 5 kPa, 010KPG = 10 kPa, 100KPG = 100 kPa, 200KPG, 500KPG, 001MPG = 1 MPa; GN = vacuum, GPN = ± ranges (see the datasheet tables). Models ending in **33** run on 3.3 V with a **0.2–2.7 V** output.

Typical uses: water level in a tank (air tube), cuff/blood pressure projects, air pumps, vacuum cleaners, pneumatics.

## Pinout (DIP6, two rows 10.0 mm apart, 2.54 mm pitch)
Pins 1–3 are on one side, 4–6 on the other (counter-clockwise, like a DIP IC; pin 1 is under 6).

| Pin | Name | Function |
|-----|------|----------|
| 1 | N/C | Do not connect |
| 2 | VDD | Second supply pin, same 5 V as pin 4 (N/C on 2019 revisions) |
| 3 | GND (Vss) | Ground |
| 4 | VDD | Supply, 5 V |
| 5 | OUT | Analog output, 0.5–4.5 V |
| 6 | GND | Second ground pin, same as pin 3 (N/C on 2019 revisions) |

This is the current CFSensor datasheet (V2.5, 2022.12). Older datasheets (2019) show pins 2 and 6 as N/C that "must be left floating": on such a part leave 2 and 6 unconnected. Pins 3, 4 and 5 alone are enough on every revision.

## Electrical limits
- Supply 5 V (the 5 V model). **6.5 V or 5 mA overload can burn the ASIC.**
- Over-pressure 2× rated (80 kPa for the 040KPG). Dry air / non-corrosive gas only, no liquids.
- Ambient −10 to 85 °C. The chip is light-sensitive: keep clear tubing out of strong light.
- Never connect pin 1 (N/C). Pins 2 and 6 are VDD/GND on the current datasheet but N/C on 2019 revisions.

## Interface details
```
P (kPa) = (Vout / VDD − 0.1) / 0.8 × 40
```
At VDD = 5 V: 0.5 V = 0 kPa, 1.5 V = 10 kPa, 2.5 V = 20 kPa, 3.5 V = 30 kPa, 4.5 V = 40 kPa.
The output is ratiometric: if the 5 V rail is really 4.8 V, pass `vs=4.8`.

## Using the driver
```python
from xgzp6847a import XGZP6847A
import time
s = XGZP6847A(34, fs_kpa=40, divider=101 / 68)  # 33k/68k divider on OUT
s.calibrate_zero()                             # port open to air
while True:
    print('P=%.1f kPa' % s.pressure_kpa())
    time.sleep(1)
```
For a ± or vacuum model pass `pmin_kpa` (pressure at 0.5 V) and `fs_kpa` (pressure at 4.5 V), e.g. 100KPGN: `pmin_kpa=-100, fs_kpa=0`. For a "33" model: `vs=3.3, lo=0.2, hi=2.7` (no divider needed).

## Gotchas
- **Check the model suffix**: the same-looking part comes in many ranges; the wrong `fs_kpa` scales every reading.
- **Power it from 5 V** (unless it is a 33 model). On 3.3 V the 5 V model's output is wrong.
- **Divider on 3.3 V boards**: OUT reaches 4.5 V.
- **Gauge pressure**: the reading is relative to room air; it does not measure atmospheric pressure.
- **ESP32 ADC** is nonlinear near its ends; use ADC1 pins (ADC2 stops with Wi-Fi).

## On a 3.3 V board
- **OUT** can rise to 4.5 V, so on an ESP32 or Pico it goes to the GPIO through a divider: 33 kΩ from OUT to the pin, 68 kΩ from the pin to GND (Sparkbench adds both resistors). The pin then sees 0.67 × the output voltage, so multiply ADC readings by 1.49 (the driver's `divider` argument does this).
